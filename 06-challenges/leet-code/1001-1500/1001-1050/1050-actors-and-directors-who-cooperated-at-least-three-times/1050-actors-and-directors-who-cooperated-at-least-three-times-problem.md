# 1050. Actors and Directors Who Cooperated At Least Three Times

**Difficulty:** Easy
**Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/actors-and-directors-who-cooperated-at-least-three-times/

---

## Problem Statement

Table `ActorDirector` has columns: actor_id, director_id, timestamp.

Write a solution to find all the pairs `(actor_id, director_id)` where the actor has cooperated with the director at least three times.

---

## Examples

**Example 1:**
```
Output: (actor_id, director_id) pairs with >= 3 cooperations
```

## Constraints

- Table as described above.
