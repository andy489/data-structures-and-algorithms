# 1349. Maximum Students Taking Exam

[Problem Link](https://leetcode.com/problems/maximum-students-taking-exam/)

## Description
Given m×n grid with '#' (broken) and '.' (seats), place maximum students such that no cheating: no two students in the same row adjacent, and no diagonally adjacent students in adjacent rows.

## Examples
Input: seats = [["#",".","#","#",".","#"],[".","#","#","#","#","."],["#",".","#","#",".","#"]] → 4

## Constraints
- m == seats.length <= 8
- n == seats[i].length <= 8
