# 1164. Product Price at a Given Date

**Difficulty:** Medium
**Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/product-price-at-a-given-date/

---

## Problem Statement

Table `Products`: (product_id, new_price, change_date).

Find the prices of all products on 2019-08-16. Assume all products have price 10 before any price change.
