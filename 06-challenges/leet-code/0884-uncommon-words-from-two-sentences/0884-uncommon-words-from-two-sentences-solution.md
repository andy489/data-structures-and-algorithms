# 884. Uncommon Words from Two Sentences — Solution

**Difficulty:** Easy
**Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/uncommon-words-from-two-sentences/

---

## Solution: Word Count — O(n) time, O(n) space

**Idea:** Count frequency of each word across both sentences combined. Words with frequency 1 are uncommon.

**Complexity:**

| | |
|---|---|
| Time | O(n) — n total characters |
| Space | O(n) |
