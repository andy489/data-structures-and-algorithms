# 1117. Building H2O

**Difficulty:** Medium
**Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/building-h2o/

---

## Problem Statement

There are two kinds of threads: oxygen and hydrogen. Your goal is to group these threads to form water molecules. There is a barrier where each thread has to wait until a complete molecule can be formed. Hydrogen threads call `hydrogen()` and oxygen threads call `oxygen()`.

---

## Constraints

- Concurrency problem; see problem page for full details.
