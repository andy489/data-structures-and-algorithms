# 1388. Pizza With 3n Slices

[Problem Link](https://leetcode.com/problems/pizza-with-3n-slices/)

## Description
Pizza with 3n slices arranged in circle. Alice, Bob, Charlie take slices in turns (you pick one, two adjacent are removed). Maximize your total sum of slices.

## Examples
Input: slices = [1,2,3,4,5,6] → 10

## Constraints
- 3 * n == slices.length
- 1 <= slices[i] <= 1000
